Before contacting support

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful when possible.

Screen Time

Scroll.Out needs Screen Time permission to apply the restrictions you select. On first launch, tap Continue and approve Apple’s system authorization. You can edit or delete a schedule before it starts.
