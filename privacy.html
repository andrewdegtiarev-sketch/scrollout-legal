Privacy Policy for Scroll.Out

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Overview

Scroll.Out does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data.

Information stored on your device

The app stores schedules, selected app or category restrictions, language preferences, and notification preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app’s functionality.

Screen Time

Scroll.Out uses Apple’s Screen Time APIs to apply restrictions selected by the device owner. The app does not access the content of other apps and does not upload Screen Time information to a server.

Analytics and advertising

Scroll.Out does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, trackers, or user accounts.

Voluntary support

If you choose to use the optional voluntary-support link, payment processing takes place on the external payment service’s website. Scroll.Out does not receive or store payment card information.

Deleting your data

You can delete schedules in the app. Removing the app deletes its locally stored app data from your device.
